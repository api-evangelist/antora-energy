# Antora Energy

> Antora Energy is a San Jose, California clean-energy hardware company that designs, manufactures and deploys factory-built thermal batteries for heavy industry, data centers and the grid. Its system resistively heats blocks of solid carbon to temperatures up to 2,400C when electricity is cheapest, stores that energy for multiple days, and discharges it around the clock as radiant heat delivered to industrial processes or paired with off-the-shelf steam turbine equipment for firm power. Antora also manufactures thermophotovoltaic (TPV) heat-to-power cells.

Antora Energy publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no SDK and no machine-readable API contract**. This file is generated by API Evangelist from public company sources; it is not published by Antora Energy.
